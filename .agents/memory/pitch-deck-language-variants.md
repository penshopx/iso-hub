---
name: Pitch deck language variants
description: Why ISO Hub investor decks are maintained as separate language artifacts.
---

Maintain the Indonesian and English ISO Hub pitch decks as separate artifacts rather than switching one deck between languages.

**Why:** Each language needs independent visible copy, presenter notes, validation, and downloadable exports. Separate artifacts preserve already-approved versions and prevent a translation update from overwriting the other audience's deck.

**How to apply:** When revising or exporting a pitch deck, make the change only in the requested language artifact. Keep slide order, visual system, and factual claims aligned across both variants unless the user explicitly requests localization beyond translation.
